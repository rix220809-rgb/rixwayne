# Today Brief Specification

## Mandatory brief types
- Period estimate: 3, 2 or 1 day before
- Period Day 1
- Every 100 relationship days
- Anniversary
- Shun birthday
- Wayne birthday
- Valentine's Day
- White Day
- Qixi
- Christmas

## Excluded from popup
- Pill reminder
- Daily question reminder
- General period days after Day 1
- Ordinary memories

## Queue behavior
1. Collect all mandatory briefs for today.
2. Sort by priority.
3. Show one card at a time.
4. The user must press `下一則` for each card.
5. The last card uses `開始今天`.
6. Each completed card is stored as seen for that date.
7. Reloading does not repeat completed cards.

## Copy tone
- Lightly humorous and affectionate
- Game-system language is allowed
- Avoid cold medical wording
- Do not use the 🤣 emoji
